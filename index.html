Frequently Asked Questions

How does MKV Studio Pro preserve 100% audio quality?

Unlike conventional converters that decode and re-encode audio into a lossy stream, our converter defaults to Stream Copy (remuxing). It extracts the exact digital audio packets (e.g. AAC, AC3, FLAC) from the MKV container and encapsulates them into the MP4 container untouched.

Why is the conversion so fast?

When video and audio streams are already compatible (such as H.264 video with AAC audio), re-encoding is completely skipped. The converter copies bytes directly at your hard drive's read/write speed (300MB/s–1000MB/s), finishing in seconds.

Do I need to install FFmpeg or Python?

No. The standalone Windows executable is completely self-contained. If FFmpeg is not already found on your computer, the app offers a one-click automatic download of the lightweight engine on first launch.

Is MKV Studio Pro free and open source?

Yes, 100% free and open-source under the MIT license with full source code hosted on GitHub.
